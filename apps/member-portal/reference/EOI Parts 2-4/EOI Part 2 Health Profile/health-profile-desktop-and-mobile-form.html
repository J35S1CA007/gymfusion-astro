Collection of Health Information Notice

Please read this notice before continuing.

Purpose of Collection

GYMFUSION collects personal and health information, including medical history, disability, and fitness-related information, for the purpose of assessing health-related risks, determining safe participation in training, and identifying any supports or reasonable adjustments that may be required.

This information enables GYMFUSION to uphold participant safety, meet its duty of care, and make informed decisions regarding program suitability.

Basis of Screening

GYMFUSION’s screening process is informed by Australia’s national pre-exercise screening framework; however, it is not a direct reproduction of the Australian Pre-Exercise Screening System (APSS).

The process incorporates gymnastics-specific, movement-based, and follow-up questions to obtain a comprehensive understanding of an individual’s background and training needs. Where responses indicate that additional support or consideration may be beneficial, GYMFUSION staff will discuss appropriate options with the participant.

Participant Responsibilities and Disclosures

Participants are required to provide accurate and complete information to the best of their knowledge, based on their current and, where relevant, past health status.

Disclosure of a health condition, injury, or relevant aspect of disability does not automatically preclude participation in GYMFUSION services. This information is used solely to determine appropriate supports or reasonable adjustments, whether a condition is stable for participation, the level of supervision or session format that may be suitable, and whether medical clearance or referral to a qualified health professional is advisable.

Where clinical guidance is recommended, participants may re-engage once appropriate advice has been obtained regarding safe participation, including any required supports or modifications. GYMFUSION adopts a person-centred approach, with a focus on supporting individuals to participate safely and effectively.

Legal Authority and Privacy Compliance

Personal and health information is collected and managed in accordance with applicable privacy laws, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), including the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), and the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic), including the Health Privacy Principles (HPPs), where applicable.

Because GYMFUSION collects and handles health information in Victoria, that information is handled in accordance with the Health Records Act 2001 (Vic) and the Health Privacy Principles (HPPs), where applicable.

Health information is collected and used solely for the purpose of assessing risk and supporting safe participation in GYMFUSION programs, unless otherwise required or authorised by law.

Storage, Retention, and Disposal

Health information collected as part of the assessment process will be retained as part of the participant’s Expression of Interest record.

Where an individual becomes a member, relevant information will form part of their ongoing member record and be retained in accordance with operational and legal requirements.

Where an individual does not proceed, their information will be securely destroyed or de-identified in accordance with GYMFUSION’s Privacy Policy and applicable Commonwealth and State privacy legislation.

Use and Disclosure

Health information is used by authorised GYMFUSION staff for health screening, safety, coaching suitability, reasonable adjustments and related service administration. It may also be processed through GYMFUSION’s approved technology providers as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not disclose health information to external health professionals or other third parties unless you consent, the disclosure is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and permitted by law, or disclosure is otherwise required or authorised by law.

Access and Correction

You may request access to, or correction of, health information we hold about you. Details about how to make a request are available in our Privacy Policy or via privacy@gymfusion.com.au.
